## Unresolved rights and safe wording

- **Game artwork:** the asset register explicitly marks some images as lacking verified bundle redistribution permission. Availability on an asset site is not a redistribution license. Safe wording in CREDITS and section 6 excludes those materials from the project grant. Confirmation from the rights holder or replacement/removal should precede a future distribution decision; this task does not modify assets or existing releases.
- **Runtime recommendation data and images:** public access does not by itself establish rights to republish or monetize that data. Those providers' applicable terms and owners' rights remain separate.
- **Earlier grants:** new restrictions cannot be applied to independently granted earlier rights. Section 6 expressly preserves them; no assertion of retroactive withdrawal is made.
- **GitHub:** permission requirements do not override rights under GitHub's applicable terms, including its fork functionality. A fork is not treated as consent to unrelated monetization.
- **Media vs application revenue:** section 2 expressly permits monetized reviews/videos/streams; section 3 regulates revenue from the app and derived products, even with free downloads. The exception does not authorize code or binary distribution.
- **Authorship:** modification authors retain credit for their additions; original authorship and third-party ownership are not reassigned to kvintin.
