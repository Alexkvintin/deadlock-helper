## What's New

- **Counter Items** now has Team, Lane, Situational and Auto modes. Pick multiple lane opponents, see which items fit your current souls, and mark an item as purchased to remove it from suggestions.
- **Match Review** adds side-by-side comparisons for K/D/A, damage, healing, economy and Impact Score. Filter the item timeline by category and spot recorded sales at a glance.

## Improvements

- **Scout** follows confirmed hero changes and updates the matching portraits, tags and counter recommendations. Tactical Insights now gives short ally and opponent notes based on recorded games on the current hero.
- Switching between player profiles is more responsive. Match graphs and item history show the recorded data available for each player.

## Known Limitations

- Tested through a full Standard/Unranked match. Ranked-specific behavior and the impact on FPS and 1% lows still need validation.
- Automatic lane guidance requires confirmed lane data. You can pick opponents manually whenever that data is unavailable.
- The item timeline can be incomplete, and the final inventory remains unverified. In one validated match, a player's local objective damage differed from the public result.

## Installation

On Windows 10 or 11 x64, download `DeadlockHelper-v1.2.0-beta.1-win-x64.zip`, extract the entire archive to a folder, and run `DeadlockHelper.exe`. Keep the files together.
